## Why do startups look for Optimizely alternatives?

Optimizely is built as an enterprise digital experience platform. Its Agentic Experimentation product lists A/B, multi-page, feature and server-side testing, real-time personalization, warehouse-native analytics and a proprietary stats engine. That breadth is the point for large programs. It is also why small teams bounce off the pricing page: there is no self-serve checkout and no public tier matrix for the web testing product most people mean when they say "Optimizely."

An **Optimizely alternative**, for this article, is any hosted A/B testing tool that can do the job a startup usually bought Optimizely for: split visitors between versions, keep each visitor in one version (sticky assignment), count conversions, and report a result you can defend. Feature flags, CMS and commerce modules are out of scope unless a competitor sells them as part of the same quoted stack.

Google named Optimizely, VWO and AB Tasty as Google Analytics integration partners when Optimize shut down ([Google's Optimize sunset help page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/12979939?hl=en)). That list is about GA4 integrations, not about price. Our guide to [Google Optimize alternatives for developers](https://www.freeabtest.com/blog/google-optimize-alternatives-developers) covers the GA4 side in more detail.

## How much does Optimizely actually cost?

Optimizely does not tell you. On 7 November 2026, both its plans page and its pricing URL open with "Get pricing tailored to your business" and ask you to describe your needs for a personalized quote. Optimizely's own docs say cost varies with traffic, which products you buy and how complex the implementation is, and point you back to Plans and pricing on Optimizely.com.

Third-party buyer reports often quote annual contracts in the tens of thousands of dollars for experimentation alone. Those figures are useful as a planning range, but they are not on Optimizely's site, so we do not treat them as list prices. If someone quotes you a single Optimizely number without naming the products and monthly tracked users in the deal, ask for the SKU list.

## What should a small team check before replacing Optimizely?

Six checks, in order:

1. **A published price, or a free plan with no end date.** A 15 or 30 day trial is not a replacement for knowing next year's bill.
2. **Sticky assignment.** The same visitor should keep the same variant when they return. Our guide to [how A/B testing tools assign visitors](https://www.freeabtest.com/blog/ab-test-bucketing-hashing) explains the hash of visitor ID plus experiment ID.
3. **Statistics you can stop on.** If you check the dashboard every day, you need a test that stays valid when you peek. Our guide to [how long to run an A/B test](https://www.freeabtest.com/blog/how-long-to-run-an-ab-test) shows what daily peeking does to a fixed-sample z-test.
4. **What counts toward the limit.** Tested visitors, monthly tested users or tracked pageviews are not the same unit.
5. **How variants are built.** Code, a second URL, or a visual editor. Code and URLs get reviewed like any other change.
6. **What you will not get.** Feature flags, warehouse exports, SSO and personalization suites sit on enterprise products. Do not buy a cheap A/B tool expecting those to appear later for free.

**Unbounce** Experiment at $149 a month ($112 billed annually) adds unlimited A/B testing on Unbounce-hosted landing pages, up to 30,000 visitors. Use it when the pages you test live inside Unbounce. It is not a site-wide A/B tool for an app you already host elsewhere.

## When is Optimizely (or Wingify) still the right choice?

Stay with Optimizely, or get a Wingify quote, when at least two of these are true:

- You need feature flags and server-side experiments in the same platform as web A/B tests.
- You need personalization, warehouse-native analytics or a CMS/commerce suite under one contract.
- Legal or security needs a named SLA, SSO, data residency options and a redlined DPA before anyone pastes a snippet.
- Your program already runs dozens of concurrent tests across brands, and a visual editor at the entry tier is non-negotiable.
- Procurement requires a sales process; a published $10 or $99 plan will not clear finance.

Wingify is the brand behind VWO and AB Tasty after [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/20/everstone-combines-wingify-and-ab-tasty-for-100m-digital-experience-optimization-platform/) the January 2026 combination. On 7 November 2026, Wingify's pricing page lists Growth, Pro and Enterprise for web experimentation with Schedule a Demo buttons and no dollar amounts. Treat VWO and AB Tasty as the same sales-quote category as Optimizely for budget planning.

## How do you pick an Optimizely alternative?

Answer three questions.

**1. Do you need enterprise modules today?** Feature flags, personalization, warehouse analytics, SSO and a named SLA point to Optimizely or Wingify. Plain sticky A/B or split URL tests point to a published-price tool.

**2. Can you start this afternoon?** freeabtest.com, Crazy Egg, Convert, Kameleoon (trial) and Unbounce publish a self-serve path. Optimizely and Wingify need a demo.

**3. How much traffic do you actually have?** No tool shrinks the sample a test needs. At a 3% baseline, detecting a 20% relative lift still needs about 13,914 visitors per variant at 95% confidence and 80% power. Our [sample size guide](https://www.freeabtest.com/blog/ab-test-sample-size) works the formula, and our [confidence interval guide](https://www.freeabtest.com/blog/confidence-interval-ab-test) shows why a single winner number is not enough.
